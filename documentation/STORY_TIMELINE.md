# Saint Veyra — Story & Timeline

## Premise
Elias Morel, a freelance investigative journalist, receives an anonymous audio
recording in his missing sister **Lena**'s voice: *"Do not let them open the lower
ward."* Lena vanished three years ago while investigating **Saint Veyra Hospital**.
Elias drives out during a storm; the entrance collapses behind him.

## The truth
Hospital director **Doctor A. Voss** ran an illegal programme to *extract* traumatic
memories from patients and store them in a resonant **lattice** beneath the hospital.
The extracted fear did not dissipate — it **cohered** into a physical creature that
wears an attendant's uniform and a half‑formed face: **The Hollow Attendant**.

Lena infiltrated as a journalist, was forced into the programme, and let the
Attendant take her fear so she could reach the lattice and stop the final extraction.
The largest part of the creature is now *hers*.

## Timeline

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| Years prior | Saint Veyra operates as a psychiatric hospital; Voss begins "the Programme". |
| Programme, mid | Front‑desk admissions suspended; "Programme" patients routed to the lower ward. The lattice begins generating instead of storing. |
| Programme, late | Staff report a tall figure in attendant's whites; the lattice draws power on its own. Lockdown revised to require **three** access seals. |
| 3 years ago | **Lena** enters as press, refuses to leave, is enrolled by Voss. She finds the procedure logs, records her logs, and gives her fear to get close to the lattice. |
| The fire | A fire originates in Sub‑Level B. Six staff and eleven patients vanish — no remains. The lower ward is sealed; the site condemned. Voss is killed by the Attendant. |
| Now | Elias receives Lena's message and enters during a storm. |

## Delivery in‑game
- **Documents** (read in the world, kept in the journal): intro letter, electrical
  map, admission log, staff warning, fire/incident report, security memo, Voss's
  journal, procedure log B‑17, containment notes, Lena's patient intake, the ward
  calendar (keypad clue), Lena's note.
- **Audio logs** (timed transcripts): Lena's anonymous message, Voss's two
  dictations, the night guard's last patrol, Lena's log, **Lena's final recording**.
- **Environmental**: collapsed entrance, blood traces, the wheelchair that moves,
  CCTV figure, red emergency lighting, the backward announcement.

## Evidence → endings
Collecting all five key pieces — **Voss's journal, the experiment log, Lena's final
recording, the containment notes, and Lena's patient intake** — unlocks the
**Containment** procedure at the device.

- **A — Release:** destroy the machine, loose the memories, escape as Saint Veyra
  collapses. Elias survives, alone.
- **B — Reunion:** activate the machine believing Lena can be saved; the Attendant
  surfaces through her and steps out wearing her face.
- **C — Containment:** perform the correct ritual (Eye → Spiral → Broken Circle:
  *watched, remembered, undone*) and seal the lattice from the inside. The creature
  is contained; Elias stays with what remains of Lena.
